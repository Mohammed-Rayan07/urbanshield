Trafikon field notice TFN-2025-09

Vendor: Trafikon
Product: TS-7 traffic signal controller
CVE: CVE-2025-31337
CVSS base score 6.1

The TS-7 management service accepts unauthenticated configuration writes. Applies to the
trafikon firmware family below 7.3.0. Operators should take signal controllers offline for
an immediate update.

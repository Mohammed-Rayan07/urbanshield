VOLTGRID SECURITY ADVISORY VSA-24-011

Manufacturer: Voltgrid
Model: VG-50 EV charger
Identifier: CVE-2024-48201, CVSS 5.4

A debug service left enabled in production builds listens on TCP 2323 and accepts a
hard-coded maintenance password. Chargers running voltgrid firmware before 2.1.0 are
affected. Firmware 2.1.0 disables the service. Chargers often bridge the charging network
and the city traffic network, so treat them as gateways when you assess exposure.

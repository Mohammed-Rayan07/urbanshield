Trafikon field notice TFN-2025-09

Vendor: Trafikon
Product: TS-7 traffic signal controller
CVE: CVE-2024-0769
CVSS base score 5.3

The TS-7 web management service is affected by a path traversal in its POST request handler.
Applies to the trafikon firmware family below 7.3.0. Operators should take signal
controllers offline for an immediate update.

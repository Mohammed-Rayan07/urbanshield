DECORLITE SECURITY NOTICE DSN-26-003

Manufacturer: Decorlite
Model: DL-1 decorative lighting controller
Identifier: CVE-2026-34909, CVSS 10.0

The DL-1 ships a bundled network management agent with a path traversal flaw that lets
anyone on the network read and change files on the controller, up to taking over its local
account. Controllers running decorlite firmware before 1.1.0 are affected; 1.1.0 removes the
agent. DL-1 units only drive festive and facade lighting and have no network links to other
city systems.

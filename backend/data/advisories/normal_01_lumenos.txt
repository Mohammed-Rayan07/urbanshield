Lumenos Product Security Bulletin LSB-2025-07

Vendor: Lumenos
Product: SL-200 streetlight controller
CVE: CVE-2025-31337
CVSS v3.1 base score: 6.1 (Medium)

Summary: The management port of SL-200 controllers accepts unauthenticated configuration
writes. An attacker on the same network segment can change dimming schedules or pivot to
other devices on the lighting network.

Affected firmware: lumenos, all versions prior to 4.2.0.
Fixed in 4.2.0. Until you can update, restrict the management port to the operations VLAN
and rotate the controller credentials.

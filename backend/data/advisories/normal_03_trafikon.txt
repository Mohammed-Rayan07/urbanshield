Trafikon field notice TFN-2023-04 (republished)

Vendor: Trafikon
Product: TS-7 traffic signal controller
CVE: CVE-2023-29910
CVSS base score 4.3

The TS-7 web console does not expire sessions, so a stolen session cookie stays valid
until the controller reboots. Applies to the trafikon firmware family below 7.3.0.
Version 7.3.0 adds a 15-minute idle timeout.
